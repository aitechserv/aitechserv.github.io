CardioLog — BP & Sugar Tracker

Track blood pressure and blood sugar for your whole family.

CardioLog lets you log readings, run guided BP sessions, and generate doctor-ready PDF reports — with separate profiles for each family member. It works 100% offline with no account needed, so health records stay private on your device.

Guided BP sessions

Take multiple blood-pressure readings with built-in rest timers when you want a more consistent, reliable average.

Doctor-ready reports

Generate a clear PDF of readings and trends to support a productive conversation with your healthcare professional.

Family profiles

Track parents, a partner, or yourself — each person keeps their own separate readings, trends, and reports.

Weekly insights

See changes and logging consistency at a glance, without navigating a complicated medical dashboard.

100% offline & private

Readings, profile names, notes, and exported reports stay stored locally — never sent to analytics or diagnostic services.

Temperature Monitoring

Real-time temperature monitoring for any environment.

Wireless IoT sensors track temperature continuously across cold chains, warehouses, server rooms, greenhouses, and laboratories — with instant alerts the moment a reading drifts out of a safe range, so you can act before stock or equipment is at risk.

Continuous sensing

Battery-powered wireless sensors log temperature around the clock and stream readings to a central dashboard.

Threshold alerts

Receive SMS, email, or app notifications the instant a reading crosses the limits you define for each location.

Multi-site dashboards

Monitor every site and sensor from one screen, with live status, history, and trend charts.

Compliance reporting

Export audit-ready temperature logs to support food-safety, pharmaceutical, and quality standards.

Humidity Monitoring

Keep humidity in the right range, everywhere it matters.

IoT humidity sensors for warehouses, agriculture and greenhouses, museums and archives, laboratories, and manufacturing — protecting stock, crops, and sensitive materials from moisture, condensation, and drying damage.

Precise RH tracking

Measure relative humidity continuously with calibrated sensors built for demanding environments.

Environment presets

Tailored safe ranges for storage, growing, archival, and production settings — configured to your needs.

Early-warning alerts

Get notified before condensation, mould, or excessive drying can cause costly damage.

Combined climate view

Pair with temperature sensors for a complete environmental picture across every site.
